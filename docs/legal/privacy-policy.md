# Guardians — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-12_

Guardians ("we", "us", the "app") helps a community rescue feral and lost cats by
letting people report sightings on a map, claim and complete rescues, and place
cats in adoptive homes. This policy explains what we collect, why, and who we
share it with.

## What we collect

- **Account data** — your email/auth identifier, username, and profile details.
- **Location** — the location of a sighting you report, and (if you opt in to
  urgent-help notifications) a coarsened "home area" used to notify you about
  nearby urgent sightings. Precise coordinates are never shown to other users.
- **Photos** — images you upload for a sighting or your profile.
- **Content you write** — report titles/descriptions, rescue updates, comments.
- **Usage/diagnostic data** — in-app product events and (optionally) crash
  reports, used to operate and improve the app.

## How we use it

We use your data to run the core service (map, rescues, adoptions, gamification),
to send the notifications you opt into, to keep the community safe (moderation and
abuse prevention), and to understand and improve how the app is used.

## Who we share it with (sub-processors)

We share limited data with service providers that process it only on our behalf:

| Processor                  | Data shared                                 | Purpose                                                        |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Supabase                   | Account, location, photos, content, usage   | Hosting: database, authentication, file storage                |
| Expo                       | Device push token                           | Delivering push notifications                                  |
| Google Maps                | Approximate viewport / sighting coordinates | Rendering the map                                              |
| Sentry (optional)          | Crash diagnostics                           | Error reporting                                                |
| **Anthropic (Claude API)** | **Cat photos and report text you submit**   | **AI features (photo-to-report autofill, content moderation)** |

### AI features and Anthropic

Some features use AI. When you use them, the relevant **cat photos and report
text** are sent to **Anthropic's Claude API** to generate suggestions (for
example, autofilling a report's color, marks, and description from a photo) and to
help screen content for safety. This processing happens **server-side only** —
the AI never runs on your device and no AI key is stored in the app.

This data is processed under **Anthropic's commercial terms with zero data
retention**: it is used only to return a result for that single request, is **not
retained** by Anthropic afterward, and is **never used to train AI models**. AI
output is always a **suggestion you review and confirm** — it never auto-submits a
report, auto-bans a user, or takes any irreversible action on your behalf, and it
does not provide medical or veterinary diagnosis.

## Your choices and rights

You can delete your account from within the app, which permanently removes your
account and associated data. You can opt out of urgent-help notifications at any
time. You may contact us to exercise applicable data rights.

## Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your
account, associated records are removed (see above).
