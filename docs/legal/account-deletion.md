# Delete your Guardians account

You can request deletion without signing in or reinstalling Guardians.

## In the app

Open Settings, choose **Delete account**, and confirm. This permanently removes
your account and its data from active systems. If cleanup fails, retry; the app
does not report success for an incomplete deletion.

## If you cannot use the app

Email **rafayetq@gmail.com** from the address registered with Guardians, using
the subject **Guardians account deletion request**. Include your username if you
know it. We verify account ownership before erasure; an email request alone does
not authorize deletion of someone else's account.

Never send passwords, sign-in links, ID documents, or private coordinates by email.

## Data removed and retained

Deletion removes your account, profile, authored reports and comments, uploaded
photos and screening documents, push registrations, rewards, and linked usage
records from active systems. Other people's community records may remain without
your account association. Backups and providers follow their applicable retention
policies. There is no account restore operation in the app.

Contact the same address for status or help with a failed deletion.
